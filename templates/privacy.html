Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep your financial information safe.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

01Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run Spendly:

  • Account details — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and log information needed to keep the Service secure and working.

We do not ask for, and you should not enter, bank account numbers, card details or other payment credentials.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Keep the Service secure and fix problems.
  • Send essential messages about your account, such as password resets.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers, and passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and expense records, except where we are required by law to keep certain information. No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

04Third Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers, for example for hosting or sending email, to help operate Spendly. They may process your data only on our behalf and only to provide those services. We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

05Your Rights

You can access, correct or delete your personal data at any time, and you can withdraw your consent by closing your account. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

06Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at privacy@example.com. You can also read our Terms and Conditions.
